## A little about me

Hey! I'm Carlos, a videogame development student who loves figuring out how games work and, more importantly, making them.

I'm particularly interested in gameplay programming, designing interesting mechanics, and creating experiences that leave something behind for the player.

Most of my time goes into experimenting with Unity and C#, building prototypes, joining game jams, and occasionally getting carried away with an idea that started as a small project.

A few things about me:

-  I'm currently working on **[Mana Zone](https://blackcreatorx.itch.io/manazone)**, a tactical magic dueling game.
-  I'm currently learning **Unreal Engine 5 and C++**.
-  I enjoy working on gameplay mechanics, combat systems, and interactive storytelling.
-  Always happy to meet other developers and collaborate on indie games and creative projects.
-  Feel free to ask me about Unity, C#, gameplay programming, or game jams.
-  One of my biggest dreams is to work in the AAA game industry.

##  Some things I've been working on

I enjoy experimenting with different genres and mechanics. Here are a few projects I've been part of!

### ✨ Mana Zone
**A tactical magic dueling experience.**

A PC game prototype where players engage in magical duels, manage their mana, counter enemy attacks, and experiment with different spell combinations.

[![Play on itch.io](https://img.shields.io/badge/Check%20it%20out-itch.io-fa5c5c?style=for-the-badge&logo=itchdotio&logoColor=white)](https://blackcreatorx.itch.io/manazone)

### 🐈 Can Someone Save My Kitties
**Save the kitties. Break the city.**

An arcade driving game made during a game jam. Race against the clock, rescue adorable cats, and get them safely to the vet.

🏆 **2nd Place — UNiAT Game Jam 2026**

[![Play on itch.io](https://img.shields.io/badge/Play%20the%20game-itch.io-fa5c5c?style=for-the-badge&logo=itchdotio&logoColor=white)](https://loneretr0.itch.io/can-someone-save-my-kitties)

### 🃏 The Wildcard: Tales of Underworld
**Survive. Adapt. Keep moving.**

A survival bullet-hell project inspired by roguelite games. Focused on fast-paced combat, enemy encounters, and player progression.

[![Explore the game](https://img.shields.io/badge/Explore%20the%20game-itch.io-fa5c5c?style=for-the-badge&logo=itchdotio&logoColor=white)](https://blackcreatorx.itch.io/the-wildcard-tales-of-underworld)
